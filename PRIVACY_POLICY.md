# Privacy Policy — Business Void

**Last updated:** 2026-08-17

This privacy policy describes how **Business Void** ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

## 1. Summary

Business Void is an offline-first idle/management game. **We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to play.** The app can be played entirely as a guest.

The only third-party services that may collect or process device data are:
- **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)** — used to serve rewarded video ads.
- **Apple iCloud Key-Value Store** — used to sync your save file across your own Apple devices (iOS only, optional).

No analytics SDKs, no crash reporters, no login providers, and no behavioral profiling are integrated.

## 2. Information we collect

**We do not directly collect any personal information.** The app does not send your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any entered text to us or to any server we operate.

The following is stored **locally on your device** (in the app's private storage) and never transmitted to us:
- Game save data (money, businesses, investments, settings, statistics).
- A locally-generated sequential "account number" used only for in-game flavor. It is not a real account and is not sent anywhere.

## 3. Third-party services

### Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
The app may display **rewarded video ads** provided by Google's AdMob network. Google and its partners may collect device identifiers and usage information to deliver and measure ads. This may include:
- Device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android).
- Coarse location (country-level) derived from IP.
- App interaction events related to ad delivery.

Google's use of advertising data is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). On iOS 14.5+, you will be asked via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt whether you consent to tracking for ad personalization. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized based on your activity.

The app requests **non-personalized ads** by default.

### Apple iCloud Key-Value Store (iOS only)
If you have iCloud enabled, the app stores a single small save blob (under 1 MB) in your private iCloud Key-Value Store so your progress syncs across your own Apple devices. This data:
- Is tied to your Apple ID and stored in **your** private iCloud container.
- Is **not accessible to us** — we cannot read, view, or recover your iCloud data.
- Does not count against your iCloud storage quota (KV store has a separate 1 MB cap).

You can disable iCloud sync at any time by signing out of iCloud or disabling iCloud Drive for the app in iOS Settings.

## 4. Data storage and security

- Local save data is stored in the app's private sandbox on your device. It is not encrypted by the app but is protected by the operating system's app sandbox.
- iCloud sync data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple's iCloud infrastructure.
- We do not transmit, store, or have access to your save data.

## 5. Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience. The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction). AdMob is configured to serve ads appropriate to a general audience. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can investigate — though as noted above, the app does not collect personal information directly.

## 6. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal information on our servers, most data-rights requests (access, deletion, correction) can be fulfilled directly on your device:
- **Delete your data:** Use the in-app **Settings → Reset Progress** button, or uninstall the app.
- **Delete iCloud sync data:** Sign out of iCloud, or disable iCloud Drive for the app in iOS Settings.
- **Opt out of personalized ads:** Decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS, or disable "Personalized Ads" in Android Settings → Ads.
